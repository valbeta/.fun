4Q2I — Four Questions to Meaningful Innovation

A filter, not a funnel: four questions every idea must survive before it earns the right to be built — and one integration that turns answers into innovation.

Q2 / 可想Where are we going?想象与可能 · Can we imagine?
Q3 / 可赞Who is with us - and why?采纳与分歧 · Can we gain adoption?
Q4 / 可行How do we get there?执行与系统 · Can we execute?

The New Loop Theory

Disruption is not a product event — it is the moment a new causal loop starts compounding while the old loop decays. Innovation = loop design.

颠覆不是产品事件，而是「新回路开始复利、旧回路开始衰减」的那个时刻。创新即回路设计。
