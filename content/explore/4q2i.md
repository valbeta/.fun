## A filter, not a funnel

Before building anything, ask four questions: where do we stand, where are we going, who is with us, and how do we get there?

The answers are not a prediction. They are a shared surface for making better decisions.

```ts
const questions = ["problem", "change", "loop", "compound"];
const decisionSurface = questions.filter(Boolean);
```
