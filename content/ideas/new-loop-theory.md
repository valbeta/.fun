## The working hypothesis

Disruption is not a product event. It is the moment a new causal loop starts compounding while the old loop decays.

Innovation is loop design: finding the smallest intervention that changes what becomes possible next.

## Open questions

- How do we measure loop velocity early?
- Which signals show adoption before growth?
- What does a healthy old-loop decay look like?
